## What the repository already implements

The endpoint, the parameters and the shape of the answer are the ones the crawler already knows — `WbCatalogApi` builds the request, `WbCatalogRequest` owns the pagination and `WbCatalogParser` turns the answer into items. The proposal therefore adds no second adapter: it pins the existing one to the evidence, so a change in the crawler and a change in the site cannot drift apart unnoticed.
